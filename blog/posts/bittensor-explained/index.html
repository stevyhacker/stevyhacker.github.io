Bittensor, explained

How Bittensor pays people for useful AI work: the chain, subnets, miners and validators, and how new TAO gets shared out.

Published Oct 03, 2026 AI · Crypto
Back to blog Open the explainer full screen ↘

I made a short animated explainer of how Bittensor works. It starts with the chain and its token, then goes into subnets, miners and validators, how Yuma Consensus turns validators' scores into rewards, and how dynamic TAO decides which subnets get the most new TAO.

It runs about three and a half minutes with narration. Figures are as of October 2026, checked against the Bittensor docs and Taostats, and it's not investment advice. Part two looks at the subnets themselves and which ones are winning.
