Inside Bittensor subnets

Part two of the Bittensor explainer: how subnets launch and stay alive, six of the leading ones, and how their revenue compares with what the network pays them.

Published Oct 03, 2026 AI · Crypto
Back to blog Open the explainer full screen ↘

This is part two of the Bittensor explainer. Part one covers how the network works. This one is about subnets: what it costs to launch one, how they get pushed out, which levers their owners have, and six of the leading ones: Chutes, Lium, Targon, Score, Affine and Templar.

The last part compares what subnets earn from paying customers with the value of the new TAO the network gives them, which is still more than ten times larger. Figures are as of early October 2026, from CoinGecko, Taostats and the SubConnect revenue index. The revenue numbers are self-reported and unaudited, and none of this is investment advice.
